Get your Facebook conversations export

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Facebook lets you download a copy of your Messenger conversations. Here is how to get it in the right format, on a computer or on iPhone, so you can import it into Reminds.

  • On a computer
  • On iPhone

Before you start

  • You need your Facebook password and access to your mailbox.
  • A computer is easier: the export can weigh several gigabytes.
  • The four settings that matter: Messages only, All time, JSON format, Higher quality.
  • Names, addresses and photos in these screenshots are hidden or replaced with examples.
  • Some screenshots are in French: the buttons are in the same place in English.

1. On a computer

On facebook.com, in a browser. Screens may change slightly between Facebook versions.

  1. Open your profile menu

    On facebook.com, click your profile picture at the top right, then “Settings and privacy”.

  2. Open Settings

    Click “Settings”.

  3. Go to your Meta Account

    In the left column, under “Your account”, click “Meta Account”.

  4. Your information and permissions

    Click “Your information and permissions”, then “Export your information”.

  5. Create an export

    Click “Create export”.

  6. Choose your Facebook profile

    Select the Facebook profile that holds your conversations (not Instagram or WhatsApp).

  7. Export to your device

    Choose “Export to device”: you will download the file yourself.

  8. The summary: four settings to change

    The summary shows what will be exported. Change the four framed lines, one at a time: information, date range, format and quality.

  9. Keep only your messages

    In “Customize information”, if everything is ticked, click “Clear all”, then tick “Messages” only. Click “Save”.

  10. The whole history

    In “Date range”, choose “All time”, then “Save”. Otherwise only the last year is exported.

  11. JSON format

    In “Format”, choose “JSON”, then “Save”. This is essential: Reminds reads JSON, not HTML.

  12. The best quality

    In “Media quality”, choose “Higher quality”, then “Save”. Your photos and videos keep their quality.

  13. Start the export

    Check the four lines (Messages, All time, JSON, Higher quality), then click “Start export”.

  14. Confirm with your password

    For your security, Facebook asks for your password again. Enter it, then “Continue”.

  15. The export is pending

    The export shows as “Pending”: Facebook is preparing your files. Depending on the size of your history, this takes from a few minutes to several hours. You can close the page.

  16. A first confirmation e-mail

    Facebook sends you an e-mail saying your download request is in progress. Nothing to do yet.

  17. The “files ready” e-mail

    When the export is ready, a second e-mail arrives. Click the “Export your information” link.

  18. Download the file

    Under “Available downloads”, click “Download”. Facebook may split the export into several zip files: download them all, then import them one by one into Reminds. They stay available for 4 days.

2. On iPhone

In the Facebook app for iPhone. The last part opens in the app’s built-in browser.

  1. Open the menu

    In the Facebook app, tap the menu (three lines).

  2. Settings and privacy

    Scroll down and tap “Settings and privacy”.

  3. Settings

    Tap “Settings”.

  4. Meta Account

    Under “Your account”, tap “Meta Account”.

  5. Your information and permissions

    Tap “Your information and permissions”.

  6. Export your information

    Tap “Export your information”.

  7. Log in again if asked

    The page opens in the app’s built-in browser. Facebook may ask you to log in again: enter your e-mail or phone number and your password, then “Log in”.

  8. “Not now”

    If Facebook offers to save your login info, tap “Not now” (“Plus tard” on the screenshot).

  9. Create an export

    Tap “Create export”.

  10. Choose your Facebook profile

    Tap your Facebook profile (not Instagram or WhatsApp).

  11. Export to your device

    Tap “Export to device”.

  12. Four settings to change

    By default, Facebook offers all information, the last year, HTML format and medium quality. Change the four framed lines.

  13. Untick everything

    In “Customize information”, tap “Clear all”.

  14. Tick Messages only

    Tick “Messages” only, then “Save”.

  15. All time

    In “Date range”, choose “All time”, then “Save”.

  16. JSON

    In “Format”, choose “JSON”, then “Save”.

  17. Higher quality

    In “Media quality”, choose “Higher quality”, then “Save”.

  18. Start the export

    Check: Messages, All time, JSON, Higher quality. Tap “Start export”. Facebook may ask for your password again.

  19. Confirmation e-mail

    A first e-mail confirms the request is in progress. Wait.

  20. The notification

    When the export is ready, Facebook sends you a notification.

  21. Download the files

    Open the “files ready” e-mail and tap “Export your information”, then “Download”. On iPhone, the zip lands in the Files app. If there are several files, download them all (you have 4 days).

3. End-to-end encrypted chats Experimental

Messenger encrypts some chats end to end. Their messages may be missing from the export above. Facebook offers a separate download, which Reminds reads on an experimental basis.

  1. Restore your chat history

    On a computer, open Messenger (facebook.com/messages). If Facebook asks, restore your encrypted chat history: click “Send code” to get a one-time code in the Messenger or Facebook app on your phone, or enter your PIN.

  2. Download your secure chats

    In Messenger settings, open “Privacy & safety”, then “End-to-end encrypted chats”, “Message storage” and “Download secure storage data”. The exact labels may vary between versions.

  3. Import this file into Reminds

    Import it like the others, after the standard export.

4. Then in Reminds Coming soon

Importing from the app is in its final round of testing. As soon as it opens:

  1. Open Reminds

    On the web or on iPhone, sign in with your e-mail address.

  2. Tap “Import”

    Pick the zip file downloaded from Facebook.

  3. Choose your conversations

    Reminds lists the conversations found in the zip: pick the ones to keep and tell it which participant is you.

  4. Several files?

    Import each zip, one at a time. Reminds adds what is missing and never deletes anything.
