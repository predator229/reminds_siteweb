Features

Years of messages, finally readable.

A Facebook export is a folder of raw files. Reminds puts it in order and makes it a pleasure to reread, like a book you can open at any page.

  • Messenger export import

    Your Facebook export (JSON format) becomes a complete conversation, with its photos, GIFs and voice messages. Importing from the app is coming soon.

  • Search

    One word is enough, even without accents. Each result opens the message right where it sits in the thread.

  • Photos and GIFs

    A gallery of everything you shared, with a direct link to the moment it was sent.

  • Voice messages

    Play voice messages again, right inside the conversation.

  • Calendar

    Every day you talked is marked. Tap a date and the thread opens on that very day.

  • Statistics

    Who writes the most, the chattiest months, favourite times of day, the longest streak.

  • On this day

    What you were writing to each other on the same date, in years past.

  • Bookmarks

    Keep the messages that matter, with a note. The app also remembers where you left off.

Privacy

Your memories stay yours.

A conversation also holds someone else’s words. Reminds is designed so it never leaves the circle of people invited to it.

Read the privacy policy

  • Access on approval

    No open sign-up: you request access in the app and the publisher approves it, or someone shares a conversation with you. Sign in with a 6-digit code sent by e-mail, or with your Google account.

  • Encrypted in transit

    Everything between Reminds and the server goes over HTTPS. Photos and voice messages are only served with a valid session, never through a public link.

  • Privately hosted

    Your data lives on a server run by the publisher. It is never read, analysed or sold. No ads, no trackers.

  • Face ID lock on iPhone

    The iPhone app locks and opens with your face. Recognition happens on the iPhone itself: nothing leaves the device.

Access

On iPhone and the web, in three steps.

Access on approval: you request access in the app, the publisher approves it and lets you know by e-mail. Or someone shares a conversation with you directly. Then it’s the same account on the web and on iPhone.

  • Installable web app

    On a computer or a phone, right in the browser. Install it from the browser to open it like any other app: it keeps itself up to date.

  • iPhone app

    With Face ID, and offline reading of what you have already opened. Installed privately for now: it is not on the App Store yet.

  1. Request access

    In the app, enter your e-mail address, your name and, if you like, a message. The publisher reviews the request and lets you know by e-mail.

  2. Coming soon

    Import your Facebook export

    Download your Facebook information (Messages, JSON format), then upload the zip from the web app or the iPhone. Import is in its final round of testing.

  3. Share it with the other person

    Share a conversation with the person who lived it with you: they get an invitation by e-mail and find it on their side.
