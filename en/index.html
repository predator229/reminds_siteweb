Where your export goes

  1. 1

    You upload the zip

    From the web app or the iPhone, over HTTPS. An interrupted upload resumes where it stopped.

  2. 2

    It is opened in an isolated sandbox

    An environment kept apart from the rest of the server: virus scan, then message reading.

  3. 3

    You choose what stays

    Only the conversations you tick are kept.

  4. 4

    The zip is deleted

    As soon as the import ends, or after 24 hours if it is not finished.

  • Access on approval

    No open sign-up. Sign in with a 6-digit code sent by e-mail, or with your Google account.

  • Encrypted in transit

    Everything goes over HTTPS. Photos and voice messages are only served with a valid session, never through a public link.

  • Privately hosted

    On a server run by the publisher, in the European Union. Nothing is read, analysed or sold.

  • No ads, no trackers

    No advertising cookie, no audience measurement, on the site or in the app.

  • Face ID lock

    The iPhone app opens with your face. Recognition stays on the iPhone.

  • Your rights

    A copy of your data or its deletion, at any time, by simple e-mail.

Read the terms of use

Is Reminds free?

Yes. No payment is asked: no subscription, no in-app purchase, no ads.

Why do I have to request access?

Because Reminds holds private conversations. There is no open sign-up: the publisher approves each request, or someone shares a conversation with you using your e-mail address.

Does Reminds connect to my Facebook account?

No. Reminds never connects to Facebook or Messenger, sends no messages and publishes nothing. You import the export Facebook gives you yourself.

What happens to the file I upload?

It is opened in an isolated environment, scanned for viruses, then deleted as soon as the import ends, or after 24 hours if it is not finished. Only the conversations you choose are kept.

Who can see an imported conversation?

You, and the people you share it with: 10 at most, and only people who took part in it. It is never published.

I appear in a conversation someone else imported.

Your rights apply too: write to contact@cybersds.fr to ask for access, correction or deletion. Answer within 30 days.

Are my end-to-end encrypted chats included?

They can be missing from the regular export. Facebook offers a separate download, which Reminds reads on an experimental basis. The guide explains how to get it.

Does Reminds replace my export?

No. Reminds is not a backup: keep your original export.
