Terms of use

What the service does, and under which conditions

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. The service

Reminds lets you reread, on iPhone, on Android and on the web (an app you can install from the browser), a Messenger conversation taken from your Facebook export: search, photos, voice messages, calendar, statistics, On this day and bookmarks.

Reminds sends no messages, does not connect to Facebook or Messenger, and publishes nothing: a conversation is visible only to the people it is shared with. It is not a Meta tool.

2. Access

Access is by invitation: someone who has a conversation in Reminds shares it with you, using your e-mail address. You sign in with a 6-digit code sent to that address, or with the Google account for that address: keep control of your mailbox.

Invitations are personal. A conversation can be shared with 10 people at most, and each account can send 5 invitations per 24 hours at most.

The web app opens in your browser. The iPhone app is installed privately; it is not available on the App Store yet.

3. Price

Invited people are never asked to pay: no subscription, no in-app purchase, no advertising.

4. Imported content

You only import conversations you took part in. They contain other people’s messages: you reread them for your personal use, without spreading them, and you share them only with people who took part in them.

You can ask for a copy of your data or for its deletion at any time, at contact@cybersds.fr.

5. Fair use

Prohibited: sharing your access, trying to access other people’s data, circumventing the security of the service, extracting its content in bulk, or importing unlawful content into it.

In case of breach, access may be suspended.

6. Liability

The service is provided as is. The publisher looks after it and secures it, without guaranteeing that it will be available without interruption.

Reminds is not a backup: keep your original export.

7. Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of the Republic of Benin. In case of disagreement, an amicable solution is sought first by e-mail, at contact@cybersds.fr. Failing that, the competent courts of Parakou have exclusive jurisdiction.
